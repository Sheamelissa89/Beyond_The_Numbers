Expected does not mean guaranteed.

Theoretical probability

What the model predicts. A fair coin has two equally likely outcomes, so the probability of heads is 1/2, or 50%.

Observed frequency

What happened in your experiment. Divide the number of times an outcome occurred by the total number of trials.

      03 / Change the possibilities

      The cosmic marble bag

      Build a bag of colored marbles. Every marble is equally likely to be selected. After each draw, it goes back into the bag.

      Changing the contents starts a new experiment, because the probabilities have changed.

        Try this: Make cyan half of the marbles. Does it appear on exactly half of every set of ten draws? Compare that with a thousand draws.
