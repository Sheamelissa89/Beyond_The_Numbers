04 / Learn by changing something

Make a wave. Hear a pattern.

Change frequency and amplitude. Watch the model respond, then listen to the tone you created.

A sine wave model appears here in browsers that support canvas.

Sound is off. Start with a comfortable device volume.

What am I seeing? A sine wave over a 0.01-second window. More cycles in the same time means a higher frequency. A taller wave means a larger amplitude. The animation moves slowly to make the pattern easier to see.

Try this: Compare 220 Hz and 440 Hz. Doubling frequency raises the pitch by one octave. Amplitude affects the signal strength; perceived loudness also depends on your hearing and speakers.

About Solfeggio: These presets let you compare tones associated with modern Solfeggio practices. They do not demonstrate claims about healing or special biological effects.
