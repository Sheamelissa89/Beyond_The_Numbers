Build your foundation

Small steps. Strong math.

Four ways to work with numbers. Choose a starting point, explore an example, and practice at your own pace.

Addition + Subtraction − Multiplication × Division ÷

+

Addition

Combine amounts to find their total. Start with one amount and count up.

Explore: Three stars and two more stars make five. 3 + 2 = 5.

−

Subtraction

Find what remains when an amount is taken away. Subtraction can also help us compare amounts.

Explore: Start with seven stars and take away three. Four remain. 7 − 3 = 4.

×

Multiplication

Count equal groups. Multiplication gives us a shorter way to write repeated addition.

Explore: Three groups of four stars make twelve. 4 + 4 + 4 = 12, so 3 × 4 = 12.

÷

Division

Share an amount equally among groups. These practice problems divide into whole numbers with no remainder.

Explore: Share twelve stars among three equal groups. Each group gets four. 12 ÷ 3 = 4.

Where do these ideas lead?

Addition and subtraction undo each other. Multiplication and division do too. Fractions give us another way to describe division.
