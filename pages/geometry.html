How far did it turn?

An angle measures a turn between two rays. The gold ray stays still while the cyan ray turns counterclockwise.

03 / Symmetry studio

One design. Two reflected halves.

Select tiles to build a design. With mirror mode on, each change is also applied across the vertical center line. Tiles can be selected using a mouse, touch, or keyboard.

The gold line is the reflection axis.

Try this: Turn mirror mode off and change just one tile. Is the design still symmetric? Use Copy left to right to restore matching halves.

Turning mirror mode on reflects your existing left half onto the right half.
